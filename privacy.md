# Keyvow Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

## Summary
Keyvow is a local-first personal order organizer. The developer does not operate a Keyvow account system, analytics service, advertising service, or backend database. We do not receive your order records, receipts, scanned images, or evidence files when you use the app as designed.

## Information you store
You may save order titles, vendors, order numbers, payment dates, amounts, currencies, payment-method descriptions, source links, delivery estimates, reminders, notes, line items, receipts, screenshots, scanned documents, and other evidence. This content is stored locally on your device. Imported evidence is protected with iOS file protection.

Receipts and screenshots may contain personal information. Review export previews carefully before sharing them.

## Camera, photos, files, and sharing
Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a document or capture evidence. Photo and file access is used only for content you select. OCR is performed on-device. The Keyvow Share Extension stores shared HTTPS links or text in an App Group inbox on your device so you can review them in the main app. It does not send that content to the developer.

## Notifications
Keyvow can create local notifications for dates you choose and an optional weekly digest. These notifications do not require a developer server. You can disable reminders in the app and notification permission in iOS Settings.

## Exports and sharing
CSV, JSON, ZIP, and PDF exports are created only when you request them. Exported files leave Keyvow only through a destination or share-sheet action you choose. Their subsequent handling is governed by the service you select.

## Purchases
The optional Keyvow Pro non-consumable purchase and restore process is handled by Apple through StoreKit. Apple processes purchase and App Store account information under its own privacy policy. Keyvow receives a verified entitlement state; the developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

## Data retention and deletion
Your records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting an order removes its local database records and imported attachments. If iOS prevents an attachment from being removed immediately, Keyvow keeps a local cleanup request and retries later. Exported copies are not deleted from destinations outside the app.

## Third parties and tracking
Keyvow does not include third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

## Children
Keyvow is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone through a developer-operated service.

## Changes
Any material change to this policy will be posted on the same public page with a revised effective date. If future features change how data is handled, the App Store privacy disclosures will be reviewed before release.
