# Keyvow Support

Keyvow keeps paid group-buy orders, original promises, evidence, partial deliveries, and personal review dates together on your iPhone.

## Common questions

### Do I need an account?
No. Keyvow has no account system and no developer-operated backend.

### Where is my information stored?
Orders and imported evidence are stored locally on your device. They leave the app only when you deliberately export or share them. App Store purchases are handled by Apple.

### Does OCR change my order automatically?
No. Camera or photo text recognition creates suggestions only. You choose which suggestions to apply before saving.

### Can Keyvow tell whether a seller is safe or a dispute will succeed?
No. Keyvow organizes your records and dates. It does not assess seller trustworthiness, determine payment-protection eligibility, file disputes, or guarantee recovery. Verify current rules with your payment provider.

### How do free and Pro differ?
The free version supports up to three active orders. The optional Keyvow Pro one-time purchase unlocks unlimited active orders. Existing orders, evidence, exports, backup, and restore remain available in both versions.

### How do I move data to another device?
In Settings, choose Export or Share Data and create a ZIP evidence archive or JSON manifest. On the other device, choose Restore from JSON or ZIP and review the preview before merging or replacing data.

### How do I change the language?
Open Settings → Language and choose System, English, or 简体中文. The change applies immediately and is included in future backups.

## Contact

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Keyvow version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not send full card numbers, passwords, or unnecessary receipt images.
